## Conclusions and primary/fallback recommendation

Both audited providers passed every consistency check on their sample: 100% fixture match rate and (where checked) 100% score agreement against football-data.co.uk, full 11-player starting XIs on every sampled match, and card/substitution counts consistent with the rules in force for their respective seasons (e.g. 3 subs/team in 2015/16 vs 5 subs/team in 2023).

Neither is usable as-is for a *live current-season* pipeline without a paid upgrade:

- **API-Football**: free plan restricted to 2022-2024 seasons, 100 requests/day, an empirically tighter per-minute limit (this audit throttled to ~1 call/7s after hitting a 429 at only 10 calls with no spacing). Depth data (lineups/events) costs 2 calls/match, so a full-round live audit (~10 matches) would cost ~20 calls — feasible on free tier for matchday-level use, but the season restriction blocks anything beyond historical backtesting today.
- **StatsBomb Open Data**: no rate limits, no account, but its open tranche stops at older seasons (2015/16 for Serie A) — excellent as a modeling/validation benchmark (used here to sanity-check the entity resolver and cross-source logic against a completely independent, high-quality event schema), unusable for anything current.
- **Sportmonks**: excluded from this audit entirely — free plan does not include Serie A.

**Recommendation** (recorded as ADR-2026-010): adopt **API-Football** as the primary candidate for lineup/minutes/events once a paid plan is approved (needed for current-season coverage); keep **StatsBomb Open Data** as a permanent free validation/R&D benchmark, not a production dependency; revisit **Sportmonks** only if its paid tier is evaluated against API-Football's paid tier head-to-head — no current basis to prefer it, since its free tier could not even be sampled for Italian football.
